# GymApp Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 17 September 2026**

## The short version

GymApp stores everything on your device. There are no accounts and no analytics. The one thing that leaves your phone is what you send the AI coach: when you ask it something, a summary of your training goes to an AI service so it can answer. If you never use the coach, your data never leaves your phone unless you export or share it yourself.

## What the app stores, and where

GymApp keeps your workouts, routines, body measurements, progress photos, gym profiles, coach conversations, and settings **locally on your device**. This data is included in your device's standard iOS backups (iCloud or computer backups), which are governed by Apple's own privacy terms.

We do not sell any of it, and we cannot see it. The only part that ever leaves your device is what the coach sends when you use it, described next.

## The AI coach

The coach answers questions about your training and can build routines for you. The AI that writes its answers does not run on your phone, so the coach needs a connection. It sends nothing until you send it a message or tap one of its suggestions. Opening the coach sends nothing.

Its answers can be wrong, and they are not medical advice. For pain or injury, see a qualified professional.

**What is sent each time you ask**

- Your message, and the recent messages in the same conversation, including the exercises of any routine or plan the coach showed you.
- A summary of your last twelve weeks of training: how often you train, your sets per muscle group each week, the exercises you do (weighted, bodyweight and timed) with their best and latest sets and your records, your routines and the exercises in them, the names of your gyms, the equipment your workouts used, and the habits your log shows (such as how long your sessions run and which lifts have stalled).
- During a workout: the routine, the exercise you are on, and the sets done so far.
- What you have told the app about yourself: your goal (including any target weight you set), your weekly workout target, training days, experience, session length, the equipment you have or don't have, exercises you dislike, and any injuries, with the notes you add about them.
- The names of the exercises in the app's library, the coach personality you picked, your weight unit, and the date and time on your phone.
- A random ID, created the first time you use the coach. Our server uses it only to count questions, and does not pass it on.

**What is never sent**

Your name, your Apple Account, your workout and exercise notes, your progress photos, the body weight and measurements you log, your effort ratings and rest times, and anything from Apple Health. When you speak to the coach, your voice is turned into text on your phone, and the recording never leaves it. Anything you type into a message is sent exactly as you wrote it.

The coach's **What the coach can see** page shows, word for word, what it is sent about you and your training.

**Where it goes**

To a small server we run on Cloudflare, which passes it to a third-party AI provider to write the answer. It is sent over encrypted connections. Both can process it outside your country, including in the United States.

**How long it is kept**

- Our server does not store your messages or the coach's answers. For each random ID, it keeps a daily count of questions asked and what they cost to answer. It also keeps brief error records, which contain no message text.
- The AI provider does not use it to train its models. It may keep it for up to 30 days to check for abuse, and then deletes it, unless the law requires it to keep it longer.
- Your conversations stay on your phone. You can delete any of them from the coach's history, and deleting the app removes them all.

**Your choice**

The coach is optional. Everything else in GymApp works the same if you never use it. If you stop using it, what was already sent cannot be called back, and is deleted on the timeline above.

## Apple Health

If you allow it, GymApp writes your **finished workouts** to Apple Health, and reads **heart rate** and **energy burned** while a workout is running so it can show them to you during and after the session. On Apple Watch it also reads resting energy for the same reason.

This exchange happens entirely on your device, through Apple Health. GymApp does not copy your Health data anywhere else, and it reads nothing except the types listed above. You can grant or revoke each permission at any time in the iOS **Health app → Sharing → Apps**, and refusing has no effect beyond those figures not appearing.

## Sharing

GymApp has no social features in this version: no accounts, no friends or followers, no community feed, and no way to publish anything on your behalf. Nothing is ever posted automatically.

When you choose to share a workout, the app hands the image or text to the standard iOS share sheet and you pick where it goes. What happens to it after that is governed by whichever app you chose.

## Camera and photo library

If you add progress photos, GymApp asks for camera or photo-library access. Photos you take or pick are saved into the app's private storage on your device and are used only to show them back to you inside the app. They are never uploaded anywhere.

## Analytics and tracking

GymApp contains no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. Apart from the coach, and Apple's own services such as TestFlight and the App Store, the app makes no network requests.

## Data deletion

You can erase your workouts, routines, body data, photos, and coach profile at any time with **Settings → Reset all data** inside the app. Coach conversations are deleted from the coach's history. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Our server holds nothing of yours to delete except the daily question counts, which carry no name or contact details.

## Children

GymApp is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The AI coach is not intended for anyone under 18.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of GymApp changes how data is handled (for example, an optional account-sync feature), this policy will be updated before that version ships.
