# GymApp Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 3 September 2026**

## The short version

GymApp stores everything on your device. We have no servers, no accounts, and no analytics. Your data never leaves your phone unless you export or share it yourself.

## What the app stores, and where

GymApp keeps your workouts, routines, body measurements, progress photos, gym profiles, and settings **locally on your device only**. This data is included in your device's standard iOS backups (iCloud or computer backups), which are governed by Apple's own privacy terms.

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of it. We cannot see it. There is no GymApp server for it to go to.

## Apple Health

If you allow it, GymApp writes your **finished workouts** to Apple Health, and reads **heart rate** and **energy burned** while a workout is running so it can show them to you during and after the session. On Apple Watch it also reads resting energy for the same reason.

This exchange happens entirely on your device, through Apple Health. GymApp does not copy your Health data anywhere else, and it reads nothing except the types listed above. You can grant or revoke each permission at any time in the iOS **Health app → Sharing → Apps**, and refusing has no effect beyond those figures not appearing.

## Sharing

GymApp has no social features in this version: no accounts, no friends or followers, no community feed, and no way to publish anything on your behalf. Nothing is ever posted automatically.

When you choose to share a workout, the app hands the image or text to the standard iOS share sheet and you pick where it goes. What happens to it after that is governed by whichever app you chose.

## Camera and photo library

If you add progress photos, GymApp asks for camera or photo-library access. Photos you take or pick are saved into the app's private storage on your device and are used only to show them back to you inside the app. They are never uploaded anywhere.

## Analytics and tracking

GymApp contains no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. The app makes no network requests of any kind.

## Data deletion

You can erase everything at any time with **Settings → Reset all data** inside the app, or by deleting the app, which removes all of its data from your device.

## Children

GymApp does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of GymApp changes how data is handled (for example, an optional account-sync feature), this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the change will be called out in the app.
